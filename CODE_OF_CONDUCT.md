# 💖 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Welcome to the Iconical community!

We're here to build cool things, share knowledge, collaborate, and help one another grow.

Iconical is committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for developers, contributors, maintainers, and community members of all experience levels.

We believe that creativity and collaboration work best when people feel comfortable participating, asking questions, sharing feedback, and expressing different perspectives.

**Be kind, stay curious, and remember that there's a human behind every username.**

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Iconical-maintained repositories and their related community spaces.

## Our Values

We encourage everyone to:

- **Be respectful:** Treat others with courtesy, even when you disagree.
- **Be welcoming:** Help newcomers feel comfortable asking questions and contributing.
- **Be constructive:** Focus on improving ideas, code, and projects rather than attacking people.
- **Be patient:** Everyone has different experiences, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- **Be collaborative:** Share knowledge, acknowledge contributions, and support one another.
- **Be responsible:** Consider the impact of your words and actions on others.

Humor, friendly discussions, and technical debates are welcome.

Respectful criticism is not a violation of this Code of Conduct simply because it is negative or expresses disagreement.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Provide constructive, actionable feedback.
- Accept legitimate criticism without hostility.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Avoid unnecessary disruption of discussions.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
- Follow reasonable instructions from authorized maintainers.
- Use appropriate channels for support, development, and security reports.
- Give credit where required or appropriate.

Disagreements should focus on ideas and technical decisions rather than personal characteristics or attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following conduct is not permitted:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation.
- Hate speech or discriminatory abuse.
- Threats of violence or encouragement of harm.
- Unwanted sexual attention or harassment.
- Repeated personal attacks or targeted insults.
- Doxxing or unauthorized disclosure of private information.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contribution workflows.
- Impersonation intended to deceive or harm others.
- Spam, scams, phishing, or malicious links.
- Coordinated harassment or retaliation against people who report concerns.
- Other behavior that creates a seriously hostile or unsafe environment.

Good-faith disagreements, constructive feedback, and ordinary misunderstandings should not automatically be treated as misconduct.

Maintainers may consider context, severity, repetition, and relevant evidence when evaluating reports.

## Where This Applies

This Code of Conduct applies to Iconical-maintained development and community spaces, including:

- Repository issues and pull requests.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Code reviews and commit discussions.
- Official project collaboration channels.
- Project-related community activities.
- Other spaces where participants are acting in an official Iconical project capacity.

Official spaces hosted by third-party platforms may also be subject to those platforms' own rules.

This document does not automatically govern independent communities or unofficial projects outside Iconical's control.

## Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this Code of Conduct, please report it privately.

**Community reporting email:** [support@iconical.dev](mailto:support@iconical.dev)

When reporting, please provide:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant links or screenshots, where available.
- The usernames or accounts involved.
- The approximate date or time.
- Any other context that may help us understand the situation.

Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Please do not publish private reports or personal details in public issues or discussions.

For security vulnerabilities, use [security@iconical.dev](mailto:security@iconical.dev) instead.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services.

## How Reports Are Handled

Reports may be reviewed by Iconical or authorized project maintainers.

Depending on the circumstances, we may:

- Request additional information.
- Review relevant messages or activity.
- Contact involved participants where appropriate.
- Remove or restrict harmful content.
- Issue a warning.
- Temporarily restrict participation.
- Suspend or ban a participant from applicable community spaces.
- Refer serious matters to the relevant platform or authority where appropriate or legally required.

We aim to handle reports fairly, consistently, and with reasonable care.

We may take immediate action when a situation presents a serious safety, security, or harassment concern.

Not every violation requires a warning before action is taken.

## Privacy and Confidentiality

We recognize that reporting misconduct may involve sensitive circumstances.

We aim to limit the sharing of report details to what is reasonably necessary for investigation, appropriate action, or legal compliance.

However, we cannot guarantee absolute confidentiality or anonymity in every situation.

Information may need to be shared with relevant parties, service providers, or authorities where justified or legally required.

We will not intentionally disclose private report information merely to embarrass or retaliate against someone.

## Moderation and Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate activity within the spaces they are authorized to manage.

Actions should be proportionate to the circumstances and may consider:

- The severity of the conduct.
- Whether the behavior was repeated.
- The potential impact on others.
- Available evidence and relevant context.
- Previous warnings or restrictions.
- The need to protect the community.

Moderation should not be used to silence legitimate technical criticism, disagreement, or good-faith feedback.

Maintainers themselves are also expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

Abuse of moderation privileges may be reported through the same private reporting process.

## Requesting a Review

If you believe a moderation decision was incorrect, you may request a review by contacting:

[support@iconical.dev](mailto:support@iconical.dev)

Please identify the decision and explain why you believe it should be reconsidered.

Where reasonably practical, we may review relevant information and correct an error.

A review request does not guarantee reversal of the original decision.

Abusive or repeatedly frivolous requests may be declined.

## Maintaining a Welcoming Community

Everyone can help build a better community.

You can do this by:

- Offering help to newcomers.
- Explaining technical concepts patiently.
- Keeping discussions focused and useful.
- Providing respectful code reviews.
- Recognizing others' time and contributions.
- Reporting serious misconduct responsibly.
- Encouraging collaboration rather than hostility.

Nobody needs to know everything to belong here.

We'd rather have people asking questions and learning together than feeling afraid to participate.
